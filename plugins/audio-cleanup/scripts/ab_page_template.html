Audio cleanup A/B

Every spot inside the cut where the cleaned audio drops out against the original. Pick a pipeline, then press play on any step. Pressing another step of the same spot while one plays switches at the same moment, so you compare like for like; press the playing step again to pause.

Waveforms are drawn in decibels (−48 to 0 dBFS), so a dropout shows as a dip. The orange band marks the dropout. Steps keep their real level next to the original; RE-USE and master results are loudness-matched to the original so you compare content, not volume. Highlighted words are what Whisper did not hear in the selected pipeline's result, and Whisper sometimes confuses near spellings.
